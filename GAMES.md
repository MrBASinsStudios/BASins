# 🎮 Game Development & Interactive Worlds

*Where SinsAuthors stories become playable mysteries, haunted environments, and interactive adventures.*

**SinsAuthors Universe™ Game Development** turns original supernatural worlds into interactive experiences. The goal is not simply to retell a story, but to let players explore its locations, discover hidden truths, make decisions, and experience danger through gameplay.

## 🏫 Deadly Dutch High™ — Interactive Fiction

The **Deadly Dutch High (DDH)** universe includes visual-novel and story-driven development, drawing on Gothic school mystery, supernatural events, original characters, and branching narrative ideas.

### Creative & Design Priorities

- Character-focused scenes, dialogue, relationships, and meaningful narrative decisions.
- Distinctive Gothic and anime-inspired visual identity.
- Connected lore, secrets, and environmental storytelling.
- Interactive events and multiple narrative possibilities.
- Workflows and prototypes using **TyranoBuilder** for visual-novel development.

**Status:** Ongoing writing, design, and interactive development. Unreleased story/game files are not public by default.

## 🔦 DDH: Night Shift
### Chapter 1 — The Locked Floor

**DDH: Night Shift** is a separate **top-down supernatural survival-horror game project** set within the broader DDH creative world. It combines exploration, resource pressure, eerie school environments, puzzles, and paranormal threats.

**Core prototype and planned systems:**

| System | Gameplay goal |
|---|---|
| 🔦 Flashlight & battery | Manage visibility without wasting power |
| 😨 Fear | Make supernatural pressure affect exploration |
| ❤️ Health & injuries | Give threats consequences |
| 🚪 Locked doors & keys | Reward investigation and backtracking |
| 🎒 Inventory | Track and use important items |
| 🧩 Wards & puzzles | Unlock spaces through environmental clues |
| 👁️ The Watcher | A supernatural entity designed around being observed |
| 👤 Stalker AI | Create a distinct pursuing threat |
| 🛡️ Safe rooms | Provide limited relief and recovery |
| 💾 Local save & pause | Make a browser-based prototype practical to play |

### Visual & Level Design

The intended presentation is **dark without hiding the gameplay**: larger explorable areas, readable paths, sensible wall collisions, room variety, functional doorways, interactable objects, and multiple supernatural encounters.

The goal is a cohesive horror atmosphere — not merely darkness for its own sake.

**Status:** Prototype and iterative development. Features listed above may be implemented, revised, or still in planning; they are not a public release checklist.

## 🌸 Kai's Adventures

**Kai's Adventures** is an evolving original adventure and interactive-storytelling project. Development explores characters, narrative progression, mysterious locations, and playable ways to experience its world. Its creative direction is developed separately from Night Shift.

## 🛠️ Development Focus

- **Narrative systems:** branching dialogue, cutscenes, character events, and story flags.
- **2D exploration:** maps, room transitions, collision detection, and interactions.
- **Horror mechanics:** limited resources, stealth/tension, puzzles, and entity behavior.
- **Interface design:** inventory, pause menus, saves, accessibility, and control feedback.
- **Testing:** gameplay balance, readability, performance, and iteration.
- **Cross-media worldbuilding:** connections among novels, artwork, audio, and interactive projects.
