# 📖 Authors Zumba — Community & Publishing

*A book-first home for independent authors, readers, and publishing opportunities.*

**Authors Zumba** is a separate, connected creative platform being planned for independent writers and readers. Its core mission is to give **authors and novelists** an accessible place to publish, discover books, meet readers, and build a community around written work.

While connected to the broader SinsAuthors Universe™, Authors Zumba is intended to have **its own identity and platform roadmap**.

## 📚 Books Come First

The primary focus is **books, novels, serialized fiction, and independent literary creators**. Screenwriters and scriptwriters may have a dedicated secondary category and community space, without changing the platform's book-first purpose.

### For Authors
- Create and manage an author profile and bibliography.
- Publish books or individual chapters as supported features become available.
- Organize titles by genre, series, and relevant tags.
- Share updates and connect with followers.
- Build relationships with readers and fellow writers.

### For Readers
- Discover books and emerging voices.
- Read available chapters and free content.
- Follow authors and track new updates.
- Browse genres, categories, and recommendations.
- Join discussion and community activities as those features launch.

## 🧱 Planned Development Phases

| Phase | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Core Library** | Accounts, profiles, book/chapter publishing, reading, discovery, and essential moderation | Planned foundation |
| **2 — Community** | Follows, comments, social posts, forums/groups, and richer author-reader interaction | Planned expansion |
| **3 — Creator Services** | Optional affordable memberships and enhanced author/publishing tools | Longer-term concept |
| **4 — Distribution** | Broader professional publishing services, external channels, and royalty tools where feasible | Long-term vision |

## 🖋️ Publishing & Distribution Vision

The long-term ambition reaches beyond a reading community. Future capabilities may include:

- Ebook formatting and publishing workflows.
- Manuscript/project management.
- Print-on-demand options where provider relationships allow.
- Connections to outside retailers or libraries where partnerships and eligibility permit.
- Sales information and royalty reporting for supported distribution services.
- Optional tools that make independent publishing less complicated.

**These services are roadmap concepts, not currently available distribution commitments.** Retailer/library access, print services, and payment features depend on integrations, partnerships, business rules, and implementation.

## 🛡️ Community Principles

- Keep books and authors central to product decisions.
- Give readers approachable ways to discover independent fiction.
- Provide clear genre/category organization and appropriate content boundaries.
- Build moderation and account safety into the platform.
- Make creator monetization optional and understandable.

## 🌙 Connection to SinsAuthors Universe™

SinsAuthors Universe™ supplies an independent creator's perspective on writing, artwork, software, and publishing. Authors Zumba expands that vision outward into a platform intended for many authors, not only B.A Sins titles.

Learn about the founder at [SinsAuthor.ca](https://sinsauthor.ca).
